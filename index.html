Official Texas Hold'em Hand Rankings

1. Royal Flush

A, K, Q, J, 10 all of the same suit.

A♥ K♥ Q♥ J♥ 10♥
2. Straight Flush

Five consecutive cards of the same suit.

9♠ 8♠ 7♠ 6♠ 5♠
3. Four of a Kind

Four cards of matching rank + 1 kicker.

Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ Q♣ 7♠
4. Full House

Three of a Kind combined with a Pair.

J♠ J♥ J♦ 4♣ 4♠
5. Flush

Any five cards of the same suit (non-consecutive).

A♦ J♦ 8♦ 6♦ 2♦
6. Straight

Five consecutive cards of mixed suits (Ace can be high or low A-2-3-4-5).

10♠ 9♥ 8♦ 7♣ 6♠
7. Three of a Kind

Three cards of the same rank + 2 kickers.

8♠ 8♥ 8♦ K♣ 3♠
8. Two Pair

Two different pairs + 1 fifth kicker card.

K♠ K♥ 9♦ 9♣ A♠
9. One Pair

Two cards of the same rank + 3 kickers.

A♠ A♥ Q♦ 8♣ 4♠
10. High Card

Highest card plays when no combination above is made.

A♠ Q♥ 9♦ 6♣ 2♠
